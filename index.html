The Nobori Standard

Why Chefs & Families Trust Nobori Agro

We treat food hygiene and Halal integrity as our highest priority.

100% Hand-Slaughtered Halal

Each bird is slaughtered individually by certified Muslim slaughterers following strict Islamic rites. No mechanical stunning or machine cuts.

0°C to 4°C Unbroken Chill Chain

Immediately after dressing, cuts enter rapid air-chilling tunnels to prevent bacterial reproduction and seal in succulent tenderness.

0% Added Water or Chemicals

Local markets often soak meat in water to increase weight. At Nobori Agro, we guarantee zero water soaking, zero chlorine washes, and zero bleaching.

Solar Precision Process

We don't just claim purity — we engineer it, step by step.

01

Organic Harvest

Early-morning harvest from GPS-tracked farms in Sylhet & Bandarban under strict zero-chemical protocols.

02

Solar Tunnel Drying

Parabolic solar tunnel dryers at precise 35–38°C preserve 98.4% of vitamins, enzymes, and phytonutrients.

03

Lab Certification

Every batch tested for heavy metals, pesticides, and moisture (<4%). Certificate issued before sealing.

04

Nitrogen Sealed

Milled and immediately sealed in oxygen-free, food-grade packaging with unique batch QR code.

Our Mission

Grown with purpose.
Delivered with integrity.

Nobori Agro was born from a simple conviction: Bangladesh's hills and valleys produce some of the world's most potent botanicals — yet post-harvest loss and middlemen rob both farmers and consumers of their true value.

We partner directly with 340+ smallholder and indigenous farming families in Sylhet and Bandarban, paying fair prices and building a transparent supply chain from seed to seal.

Our solar tunnel dryers replace destructive industrial heat, returning to ancient wisdom — sun-drying — but with modern precision temperature control.
